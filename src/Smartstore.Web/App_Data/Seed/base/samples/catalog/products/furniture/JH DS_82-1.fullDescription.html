The two-seater from Josef Hoffmann's Kubus series is a stylish eye-catcher in residential and business premises. Together with the Kubus armchair and the Kubus three-seater, it creates a stylish seating group for reception halls and large living rooms.Josef Hoffmann's sofa is upholstered with a shape-retaining upholstery, covered with leather and, using a special sewing technique, shows numerous squares that form an overall picture. The purely geometric form of this Hoffman design was also a pioneer of Cubism, which reached its peak at the beginning of the 20th century.

Dimensions: width 166 cm, depth 72 cm, height 77 cm, cbm: 1,50
