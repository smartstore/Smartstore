The coffee table of Isamu Noguchi once impressed even the president of the New York Museum of Modern Art. Because it was originally designed for him. The curved ash base is an elegant eye-catcher. It appears unobtrusive and is perfectly shown off to advantage through the transparent, three-sided glass plate. A Bauhaus - furniture, which today is used in many rooms with upscale furnishings as a side table. It fits perfectly in the lounge, living room and reception areas.

Dimensions: width 128 cm, height 40 cm, depth 92,5 cm
