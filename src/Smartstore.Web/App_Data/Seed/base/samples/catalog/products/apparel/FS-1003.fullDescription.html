A minimalist sneaker made of smooth leather with a light rubber sole. Also available as a limited edition in black and gold.

What this product shows: The limited edition has its own price: switch the colour and the price changes. Sizes are selected in a dropdown. Some sizes are sold out; unlike the rain jacket, they are not greyed out, but the page tells you when you select one, and the “Add to cart” button disappears.

The plugin EU Warranty Labelling shows the uniform EU labels for the statutory warranty and for a voluntary durability guarantee. Norlumera gives a durability guarantee of 3 years on these sneakers, so the label also shows the guarantee period, the manufacturer and the model identifier.
