Three-piece golf balls with a soft feel and good distance on every club. Choose a pack of 3, 12 or 24.

What this product shows: The pack size is a product variant: price and weight change with the selection. Order five or more packs and the price per pack drops (quantity discount). The hardness in the table of properties can be used as a filter in the category.

The plugin Depending prices lets you set prices that depend on the customer group, the language, the store or the customer number. For these golf balls, a club price of 8.50 instead of 9.90 has been set up for the customer group “Golf club members”. Customers in this group see the club price as soon as they are signed in, everybody else sees the normal price.
