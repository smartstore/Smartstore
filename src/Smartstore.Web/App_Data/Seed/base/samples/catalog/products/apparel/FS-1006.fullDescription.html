A T-shirt made of organic cotton. Wear it plain or have it printed with a text of your choice.

What this product shows: A product that customers personalise. With “With print” the price rises by the print surcharge and a text field appears for your text.

The plugin Rules for product attributes controls with rules when a product option is shown. Here the text field has the rule “Print is With print”: as long as “Without print” is selected, the text field stays hidden, and nobody can enter a text that would not be printed. Rules can combine several options, for example to offer a size only in certain colours.
