A lounge chair with a high back and wide armrests. Put it together the way you like it: cover, frame and extras.

What this product shows: A configurable product. Each choice changes the price: linen and leather cost extra, the black steel frame is cheaper than oak. Leather also makes the chair heavier, which affects the shipping weight. Under “Extras” several options can be ticked at once. “Matching footstool” is a product of its own: if you tick it, the footstool is added to the order at its own price.

The plugin File Manager adds tabs with documents to product pages. Here the tab “Data sheet” offers the dimensions, materials and care instructions of the chair as a PDF to download.
