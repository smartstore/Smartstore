A floor lamp with a wide arc that reaches over sofa and armchair. The light can be dimmed.

What this product shows: Each finish shows its final price directly in the selection. The brass version is a separate article: it costs more, weighs more and is made to order, so its delivery time is longer. The lamp comes without a bulb: the matching LED bulb is a required product and is added to the cart automatically together with the lamp.

The plugin General Product Safety Regulation (GPSR) shows the manufacturer and safety information required by the EU General Product Safety Regulation in a separate tab on the product page. For this lamp, the contact details of the manufacturer Lindavero and safety notes for using the lamp are stored.

The plugin EU Warranty Labelling shows the uniform EU labels for the statutory warranty and for a voluntary durability guarantee. Lindavero gives a durability guarantee of 5 years on this lamp, so the label also shows the guarantee period, the manufacturer and the model identifier.
