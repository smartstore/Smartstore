A lightly sparkling lemonade with blood orange juice from Casa Vireluna, filled into a returnable 0.75 l glass bottle.

What this product shows: A required product as a fixed deposit surcharge. The returnable bottle needs a deposit, so the Bottle deposit is assigned to the lemonade as a required product and is added to the cart automatically. The deposit is a fixed amount per bottle. Its price is shown below the price of the lemonade, so customers see the additional cost before they order. This display depends on the setting “Show additional prices with main product”, which is switched on in this demo shop.

The quantity of the deposit follows the lemonade: one deposit is kept in the cart for each bottle, so 6 bottles mean 6 deposits. The quantity of the deposit cannot be changed in the cart, and its remove button is disabled. On the order the deposit appears as its own item and is taxed at the standard rate, like the lemonade. It is not shipped and adds no shipping costs.
